# Lexium A1 card specification

Write independently authored, concise General American English learner cards. Keep the selected Oxford source headword, POS, CEFR, and order only; do not reproduce dictionary prose or examples. Example 1 makes the sense obvious. Example 2 teaches a concrete construction, collocation, preposition, countability, or register feature. Vietnamese must be natural, concise, and sense-aligned. IPA uses `/.../` notation.
